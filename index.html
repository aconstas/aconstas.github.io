About

Hi, I'm Alex. I'm a data engineer. I build data products and pipelines that pull data out of ad platforms, inventory feeds, and business systems, clean it up, and load it into tables people can query and trust.

I work at INNOCEAN USA, an automotive marketing agency that serves Hyundai and Genesis. Before that I kept production data in sync between an ERP and dozens of SQL Server databases at Taylor Farms.

I came to data from an English degree and a full-stack coding bootcamp. I still care about clear writing, plain tools, and web pages that load fast.
